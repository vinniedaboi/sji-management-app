# Deferred backlog

The MVP deliberately leaves these modules out so they do not delay a staff pilot:

- Tasks and action-item assignment
- Multi-stage approvals and request workflows
- Room booking
- IT and facilities ticketing
- Staff leave requests and cover management
- Science practical planning and lab-equipment requests
- Purchasing and procurement
- Trip management
- Advanced skills and language matching
- Email, push, and messaging notifications
- External calendar integrations
- School SSO with Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
- AI-generated briefings and semantic document search
- Native mobile applications
- Multi-school tenancy and commercial SaaS administration

Before production, prioritize managed SSO, private object storage with signed access, backups, rate limiting, monitoring, and a formal privacy/security review.
